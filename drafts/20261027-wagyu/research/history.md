# Wagyu history: research notes

Collected 2026-10-27 by a research agent; every source was fetched by URL. "Confirmed" means the agent fetched the page and quoted it. Translations from Japanese are marked "tr.". Spot-checks by me are marked **Checked by me**.

Corrections to the brief's assumptions:
- Japan never had a legal export ban on wagyu cattle or genetics.
- 1957 is the year for Japanese Shorthorn, not Polled.
- The fourth 1976 bull is listed as "Rueshaw", not Shigeshigetani.
- Emperor Meiji's 1872 meat-eating could not be confirmed from an acceptable source.

## 1. What "wagyu" means; the four breeds

- **Definition.** JETRO: "Cattle that are recognized as Wagyu are four breeds including 'Japanese Black,' 'Japanese Brown,' 'Japanese Shorthorn,' and 'Japanese Polled' and interbred hybrids of those four breeds." https://www.jetro.go.jp/en/trends/foods/ingredients/wagyu.html (confirmed)
- **Labeling rule.** MAFF quotes the 2007 labeling guideline: the animal must be one of 黒毛和種・褐毛和種・日本短角種・無角和種 or their crosses, and 「国内で出生し、国内で飼養された牛であること」 (tr.: born and raised in Japan). https://www.maff.go.jp/j/study/wagyu_iden/attach/pdf/wagyu_iden-6.pdf (confirmed)
- **Breed shares (MAFF, April 2025).** 「黒毛和種が占める割合は、９割以上（令和６年：98.4％）」 (tr.: Japanese Black is over 90%, 98.4% in 2024). https://www.maff.go.jp/j/chikusan/kikaku/attach/pdf/250428-8.pdf (confirmed)
  - The same table's 2025 column: Black 1,733k head (98.4%), Brown 22k (1.3%), Shorthorn 5.6k (0.3%), Polled 0.2k (0.0%), out of 1,761k in total.
  - Source data: 牛個体識別全国データベース (the national cattle traceability database).
- **"Wagyu" literally means "Japanese cattle."** Unconfirmed from an authoritative source; only Wikipedia states it.

## 2. Origins

- **Draft animals.** Kobe Beef GI entry: Tajima cattle "was raised as cattle for agriculture in the mountains of the Tajima region … since about 1200 years ago". https://pd.jgic.jp/en/register/entry/3.html (confirmed)
- **The Meiji shift.** Namikawa, "Breeding History of Japanese Beef Cattle" (undated; WSU-hosted, archived copy): "there had been no public demand for animal products … until the Meiji Restoration in 1868, because of religious reasons of Buddhism and Shintoism." The same paper: "About 2,600 head of cattle were imported by 1887." https://web.archive.org/web/20161127214623/https://ansci.wsu.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/386/2016/08/BreedingHistoryofJapaneseBeefCattle.pdf (confirmed)
- **Emperor Meiji eating meat in 1872.** Unconfirmed; only Wikipedia states it.
- **Crossbreeding.** Gotoh et al. 2018: "In the early 1900s, Japan started to crossbreed native cattle with imported breeds such as Braunvieh and Simmental … Ayrshire, Devon, and Shorthorn … and Holstein". https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6039323/ (confirmed)
- **The crossbreeding boom ended.** Namikawa: "the price of crossbreds fell suddenly in 1910 … the overheated period of crossbreeding had ceased within 10 years." (confirmed)
- **Recognition dates.** JMI: Black, Brown and Polled were each "certified as indigenous Japanese beef cattle in 1944". Shorthorn was certified in 1957 ("until its certification as indigenous Japanese beef cattle in 1957"). https://www.jmi.or.jp/en/info/index1.html (confirmed)
- **Registries.** MAFF: Black registration 「1918～20年に各県で登録が開始」 (tr.: began in prefectures in 1918–20). The national Wagyu Registry Association was founded in 1948. 「1957年から日本短角種登録協会で登録」 (tr.: Shorthorn registry from 1957). https://www.maff.go.jp/j/study/wagyu_iden/attach/pdf/wagyu_iden-5.pdf (confirmed)

## 3. Grading (JMGA)

- **Yield grade.** By yield estimate (歩留基準値): A is 「72以上」 (72 or more), B is 「69以上72未満」 (69 to under 72), C is 「69未満」 (under 69). https://www.jmga.or.jp/standard/beef/ (confirmed)
- **Quality grade.** Four traits are graded: marbling, meat color and brightness, firmness and texture, and fat color and quality. The final grade is 「その項目別等級のうち、最も低い等級」 (tr.: the lowest of the four).
- **BMS to quality grade.** 「『肉質等級5』はB.M.S. No.8以上、『同4』はNo.5以上、『同3』はNo.3以上、『同2』はNo.2以上、『同1』はNo.1」 (tr.: grade 5 is BMS 8+, grade 4 is 5+, grade 3 is 3+, grade 2 is 2, grade 1 is 1). (confirmed)
- **English summary.** JMI: "Beef is graded in 15 stages in a combination of Yield Grade and Quality Grade … The Beef Marbling Standard (B.M.S.) ranges in stages between No. 1 and No. 12". https://www.jmi.or.jp/en/info/index2.html (confirmed)
- **BMS adopted in 1988.** MAFF: in 昭和63年 (1988) the carcass standard moved to 「ＢＭＳ（脂肪交雑）や歩留基準値などの具体的な物差し」 (tr.: concrete measures such as BMS and the yield estimate), in response to beef import liberalization. 250428-8.pdf (confirmed)

## 4. Brands

- **Kobe criteria (bylaws, Art. 21).** Selected from Tajima Beef; "the cattle must be a heifer or a steer"; "Yield score : Score of A or B Meat quality score: Score of 4 or above."; "BMS, of level 6 and above."; "270kg—499.9kg for cow ･300kg—499.9kg for steer". https://www.kobe-niku.jp/en/contents/about/definition.html (confirmed)
- **Tajima Beef (Art. 20).** "bred and fatted in Hyogo Prefecture … bred for above 28 month and under 60 months … processed at slaughter houses in Hyogo Prefecture." (confirmed)
- **Discrepancy:** the association's criteria page lists only "Gross carcass weight of 499.9 kg or less". https://www.kobe-niku.jp/en/contents/about/criteria.html
- **Kobe GI and export.** GI protected 2015/12/22. Kobe beef "started to be exported to Macao for the first time in February, 2012" and is "less than about 0.2% of total beef consumption in Japan". https://pd.jgic.jp/en/register/entry/3.html and https://www.kobe-niku.jp/en/contents/faq/index.html (confirmed)
- **Matsusaka.** The GI covers only *Tokusan* Matsusaka Ushi: "Hyogo Prefecture-born Japanese black-haired virgin female cattle … raised … for more than 900 days". Protected 2017/03/03. https://pd.jgic.jp/en/register/entry/25.html (confirmed)
- **Omi.** "a production history of about 400 years … In 1951, 'Omi Beef Association' was founded as the first association for promoting branded beef in Japan." Protected 2017/12/15. https://pd.jgic.jp/en/register/entry/56.html (confirmed)

## 5. Export restrictions

- **No legal ban.** A 2006–07 MAFF expert panel found that restricting genetics under the Foreign Exchange Act would be 「GATTとの整合性上、困難」 (tr.: hard to square with GATT). The tool used instead was 「団体主導による輸出自粛の活動」 (tr.: voluntary export restraint led by industry groups). wagyu_iden-6.pdf (confirmed). The American Wagyu Association instead says "the Japanese Government banned further exports … in the 1990's"; treat that as the AWA's characterization.
- **Why exports stopped.** MAFF: 「平成11年以降は、団体による輸出自粛の取組に加え、平成12年の口蹄疫の発生により家畜衛生条件が停止しており、和牛遺伝資源の輸出実績はなし」 (tr.: since 1999, voluntary restraint, plus animal-health conditions suspended after the 2000 foot-and-mouth outbreak, mean no wagyu genetics have been exported). wagyu_iden-5.pdf (confirmed)
- **The 2018 case.** MAFF: 「平成30年6月、和牛の精液と受精卵の不正な輸出を図る事案が発生」 (tr.: in June 2018, an attempt to illegally export wagyu semen and embryos). https://www.maff.go.jp/j/chikusan/kikaku/kachiku_iden.html (confirmed). China as the destination is unconfirmed; MAFF doesn't name it.
- **The 2020 laws.** The Livestock Improvement Act amendment and the 家畜遺伝資源に係る不正競争の防止に関する法律 (Act on Prevention of Unfair Competition Related to Livestock Genetic Resources) 「令和２年４月17日に成立し、令和２年10月1日に施行」 (tr.: passed April 17, 2020, in force October 1, 2020).
  - Penalties: 「個人は１０年以下の懲役、１千万円以下の罰金…法人は３億円以下の罰金」 (tr.: individuals up to 10 years in prison or a ¥10M fine; companies up to ¥300M).
  - https://www.maff.go.jp/j/chikusan/kikaku/attach/pdf/kachiku_iden-3.pdf (confirmed)

## 6. How genetics left Japan

- **First US imports.** AWA: "Wagyu cattle were first imported into the United States in 1975 when two black and two red bulls were imported by Morris Whitney." The same page lists "Judo … Mt. Fuji … Mazda … Rueshaw", each "Imported in 1976 by Morris Whitney". So the AWA page itself gives both 1975 and 1976. https://wagyu.org/company/awhf/awhf-historic-museum (confirmed)
- **1990s imports.** The AWA page lists imports dated 1993–1997. It quotes Wagyu International: "A total of 221 Wagyu cattle brought genetics from the Japanese Black and Japanese Brown and they formed the basis of all Wagyu and Akaushi in USA, Canada and Australia." (confirmed; secondary quote)
- **MAFF's count.** 「平成10年までに生体247頭、精液１万３千本が米国へ輸出」 (tr.: by 1998, 247 live cattle and 13,000 semen straws had been exported to the US). 「米国に輸出された遺伝資源が増殖され、豪州へ輸出」 (tr.: genetics exported to the US were multiplied and exported to Australia). wagyu_iden-5.pdf (confirmed). Counts vary by source: AWA 221, MAFF 247, Gotoh "248 cattle".
- **Australia.** The Australian Wagyu Association "was formed on 1st February 1989". Its database cites "Japanese breeding records of 201 foundation sires and 168 foundation dams". https://www.wagyu.org.au/worlds-luxury-beef and https://www.wagyu.org.au/about-us (confirmed)

## 7. Japan and foreign "wagyu"

- **Foreign beef can't be sold as wagyu in Japan.** JETRO: "It is not possible to re-import foreign-produced beef and sell it as 'Wagyu' in Japan." Since 2007, the label needs proof of domestic birth and breed and a "10-digit individual identification number". (confirmed)

## 2025–2026 developments

- **Record 2025 beef exports.** Japan's 2025 beef exports were ¥73.1B (+12.8%) and 12,628 t. The US was the top market at ¥15.44B and 2,453 t. MAFF calls this a 過去最高 (record). https://www.maff.go.jp/j/shokusan/export/e_info/attach/pdf/zisseki-308.pdf and zisseki-280.pdf (confirmed; all beef, not wagyu only)
- **2026 so far.** January–August 2026 beef exports were ¥51.8B (+18.7% year on year). https://www.maff.go.jp/j/shokusan/export/e_info/attach/pdf/zisseki-312.pdf (confirmed)
- **The wagyu herd is shrinking:** 1,799k head in 2024, 1,761k in 2025. 250428-8.pdf (confirmed)
- **2025 review of the 2020 law (April–June 2025).** It kept the existing provisions and did not add unfertilized eggs. Transfer contracts were in use at about 80% (「８割台」), against a 100% target. https://www.maff.go.jp/j/chikusan/kikaku/attach/pdf/250428-21.pdf (confirmed)
- **Kobe port opening date:** the Kobe FAQ says "January 1, 1869"; its history page says 1868.
