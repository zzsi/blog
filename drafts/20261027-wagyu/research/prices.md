# Wagyu price trends: research notes

Collected on 2026-10-27 by a research agent from primary URLs. The agent had no web search, so every source was fetched directly. Everything below was confirmed by fetch unless marked otherwise. Translations from Japanese are the agent's.

The brief got two things wrong:
- The US dropped its 200 t country-specific quota for Japanese beef in 2020 (see §5).
- Cow/heifer A5 carcasses sell for more than steer A5 at Tokyo.

## 2. Calf and feed costs

**Calf auctions.** Japanese Black (黒毛和種) average, thousand yen per head, from ALIC table 2140. https://lin.alic.go.jp/alic/statis/dome/data2/j_excel/2140a.xlsx
- FY2021 745 → FY2022 641 → FY2023 554 → FY2024 545 → FY2025 713 → FY2026 Apr–Sep 846.
- Peak: 861 in Apr 2026.
- Series break: 「2021年４月から集計対象を変更」 (coverage changed from April 2021).

**Compound feed.** Ex-factory, weighted average across all livestock, yen/ton excluding tax, from ALIC table 7022. https://lin.alic.go.jp/alic/statis/dome/data2/j_excel/7022a.xlsx and OldDate/7022aD.xlsx
- Jan 2020: 61,065.
- FY2021 73,172 → FY2022 88,680 → FY2023 88,699 → FY2024 86,591 → FY2025 86,884.
- Jul 2026: 93,925 (+10% YoY).

**The squeeze** (the agent's arithmetic, not a source's): from FY2021 to FY2024, calves fell about 27%, feed rose about 18% and steer A4 fell. Since 2025, calves are up about 55% from FY2024 to FY2026 H1, far faster than A5 carcasses at about 8%.

**2025 US exports were a record despite the tariffs:** 「米国向けについては、４月から関税措置が導入されたものの、緑茶や牛肉に対する旺盛な需要を背景に… 過去最高」. Another cited driver: 「インバウンドによる日本食の認知度向上」 (inbound tourism raising awareness of Japanese food). zisseki-280.pdf

**2026 to date:** Jan–Aug beef exports ¥51,824M, +18.7%. zisseki-312.pdf

**Target:** ¥113.2B of beef exports by 2030 (2024 actual: ¥64.8B); US target ¥23.6B. https://www.maff.go.jp/j/shokusan/export/progress/attach/pdf/index-43.pdf

## 5. US beef context, 2025–2026

**Herd:**
- 「All cattle and calves in the United States as of January 1, 2026 totaled 86.2 million head, slightly below the 86.5 million head on January 1, 2025.」 https://esmis.nal.usda.gov/sites/default/release-files/795748/catl0126.txt
- July 1, 2026: 94.2 million.
- "Smallest since 1951": unconfirmed.

**Prices:**
- **WASDE, Oct 9, 2026:** 5-area steer price $224.37/cwt in 2025, $255.76 in Q2 2026, $237.56 projected for 2026. https://www.usda.gov/oce/commodity/wasde/wasde1026.pdf. Whether these are records is unconfirmed.
- **ERS, Oct 23, 2026:** "Beef and veal prices are forecast to increase 8.7 percent in 2026." https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/food-price-outlook/summary-findings

**Japan quota and tariffs:**
- **Side letter:** "the United States shall eliminate the 200 metric ton country specific quota for Japan and increase the 'Other countries or areas' quota to 65,005 metric tons, allowing Japan access." https://ustr.gov/sites/default/files/files/agreements/japan/Letter_Exchange_on_Beef.pdf
- **HTS 2026 Rev. 21:** "United Kingdom 13,000" plus "Other countries or areas 52,005" (the agent's sum: 65,005). In-quota rate 4.4¢/kg; over-quota rate 26.4%. https://hts.usitc.gov/reststop/file?release=currentRelease&filename=Chapter%202
- **Reciprocal tariff, Sept 4, 2025:** Japanese goods face 15% all-in, and where the MFN rate is ≥15%, "the additional rate of duty… shall be zero percent." https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/09/implementing-the-united-states-japan-agreement/
- **Nov 14, 2025:** "certain agricultural products shall not be subject to the reciprocal tariff," effective Nov 13, 2025. The annex lists beef subheadings 0201.10.05–0202.x. https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/11/modifying-the-scope-of-the-reciprocal-tariff-with-respect-to-certain-agricultural-products/

**Brazil:**
- EO 14323 (July 30, 2025): "an additional ad valorem duty rate of 40 percent on certain articles of Brazil."
- Nov 20, 2025: beef subheadings were added to the exemption annex, effective Nov 13, 2025. https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/11/modifying-the-scope-of-tariffs-on-the-government-of-brazil/

**Supreme Court:** *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump* (2/20/26): "The International Emergency Economic Powers Act… does not authorize the President to impose tariffs." https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/25pdf/607us2r12_8nj9.pdf. Confirmed from the docket listing. What replaced the struck-down tariffs is unconfirmed.

**Extra 2026 quota:** 80,000 t of Argentine lean trimmings (Feb 13–Dec 31, 2026), and 300,000 t of lean trimmings from other countries (Sept 1–Nov 30, 2026).
